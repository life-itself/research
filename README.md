We are the **Seeds of Renaissance research group**: an open, para-academic community asking how a second renaissance could come about, and what a wiser, saner culture looks like. This site is our home base. It holds what we're for, how we work, our history and our past research. New writing goes out on our [Substack](https://lifeitselfresearch.substack.com/).

## Where things are

- **New writing and recordings:** our [Substack](https://lifeitselfresearch.substack.com/).
- **The wider community:** [Seeds of Renaissance](https://seedsofrenaissance.org/), which this group is part of. Start there if you're new.
- **Reference and working material:** this site, and its source in the public [GitHub repo](https://github.com/life-itself/research).

## How to join in

Come to a [Friday call](discovery-calls.md): every week at 17:00 European time, open to all, with registration on [Luma](https://luma.com/lifeitself). If the group is a good fit for you, you can then become a member. The [charter](pattern.md) explains what membership involves.

### How to schedule a session

*For members of the research group*

See this [Google Doc for how to schedule a session, and the current schedule of talks](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14wWKfP41n3SJ2ICZzjU3OMmybd9zp5BZop12Ke2ypSg/edit?tab=t.0).

### FAQs

#### Can I come up with my own project or interest?

Yes. We're very happy for people to bring their own projects and interests to the call.

#### How many people come?

It varies: normally between five and fifteen, and more for special presentations.
