- **Read** says how far the doc was read: `full` (in full or in large part), `snippet` (search snippet only), `not read`.
- **Status**: `current` (live working doc), `historic` (record of past work), `deprecated` (superseded).
- **Comment** is for Rufus or a later reader: corrections, "still true?", what matters. Blank means no comment yet.
