## Purpose and orientation

The **Research Circle (Collective)** exists to explore paths to a radically wiser, weller world through a "second renaissance".

Put more broadly: to explore, map, and prototype pathways toward *regenerative, [integral / teal / etc] second renaissance futures*, as well as the institutions, cultures, and consciousness capable of navigating the complexity of our times.

It operates as both a **scholarly inquiry** and a **living experiment**: blending research, dialogue, and embodied practice to produce **public knowledge**, **practical models**, and **collective understanding**.

### Framing question(s)

What are the paths to a second renaissance and an awakening society?

And, flowing from that:

1. **What** is a second renaissance and the new paradigm(s) that will emerge?
2. **How** do we get there? What are the paths to that?
3. **Why** do we want to go there? (What's not working today.)

Or, more accessibly: *How can organizations, institutions, and cultural systems evolve from the current extractive and mechanistic paradigms towards a second renaissance and regenerative, life-aligned paradigms?*

Or, perhaps more "second renaissance-y": *Exploring the metacrisis – what's not working and why – and a second renaissance – the vision for renewal.*

### Objectives

1. Map key dimensions of the [metacrisis](https://metacrisis.info/): epistemic, ecological, institutional, spiritual.
2. Identify and study *emergent regenerative, integral paradigms* arising in a [second renaissance](https://secondrenaissance.net/).
3. Identify and study *paths* to those emergent paradigms through the metacrisis to a second renaissance.
4. Prototype and document *cultural and institutional experiments*.
5. Contribute to *public discourse*: be accessible, rigorous, embodied.

## Governance and coordination

The Research Collective is governed and coordinated by a **Research Stewardship Circle**, which in turn is chartered by and accountable to the **Life Itself Lead Stewardship Circle**.

There are members of the overall circle (collective) and members of the stewardship circle.

### Research Stewardship Circle: composition and roles

The stewardship circle is responsible for the governance of the Research Collective, including:

- budgeting within the Research domain
- websites such as [research.lifeitself.org](http://research.lifeitself.org), and the Substack
- publication of papers and materials on those sites or with the logo/name of the Life Itself Research Collective
- discovery calls
- membership of the collective
- chartering and oversight of sub-circles
- the research section of the forum

**Potential roles**

- **Lead Steward:** convenes, holds coherence, connects outputs to Life Itself strategy.
- **Community Curator:** manages calls, forum, and community participation.
- **Research Facilitator:** manages cadence, documentation, and research flow.
- **Scribe / Communications Liaison:** supports documentation, publication, and dissemination.

Additional:

- **Partner Liaison:** coordinates with external institutes.
- **Archivist:** ensures long-term accessibility and citation standards.

### Initial stewards

To boot up this system the initial stewards are Jonah Wilberg and Rufus Pollock.

### Sub-circle chartering

The Stewardship Circle may charter sub-circles as needed, either for a fixed duration or permanently (see the sub-circles charter below). One example:

- **Name:** Climate & Metacrisis Research Circle
- **Duration:** 6–9 months (renewable by consent)
- **Hosting entity:** Life Itself Research Collective
- **Output format:** publication(s), forum, lexicon, archive, and public dialogue

## Membership

### Circle membership and roles

- **Members**
  - Contribute to the purpose of the circle by, for example: attending sessions and giving feedback; sharing research and giving talks; sharing links and ideas; sharing the circle's work with the wider world; taking on roles in the circle.
  - Benefits: access to the research WhatsApp group; access to resources such as the library; the chance to share their work (for example brown-bag talks, via a rota); use of the title "Member of Life Itself Research Circle"; the right to propose and run sub-circles (with Stewardship Circle approval).
- **Fellows:** short-term fellows contributing specific expertise (for example climate, governance).

In addition, non-member **Guests** who agree to the community guidelines can observe and contribute to discussions on the forum (we work in the open), attend the biweekly discovery calls, and apply to become members.

### Membership terms

We want people to choose to be members rather than default into it. So there is a one-year term, renewable at the member's request.

- Membership runs by calendar year, for a given year.
- By default it is renewed if the member asks for it to continue.
- All existing members of the WhatsApp group are automatically appointed General Members (unless they opt out), with membership running until 31 Dec 2026.

### Membership process

Membership is approved by the Research Stewards Circle.

| Biweekly | Research Stewards Circle | Planning and strategy |
| Quarterly | Reflection call | Review insights, adjust direction |
| As agreed | Sub-circle calls | As agreed for the sub-circle |
