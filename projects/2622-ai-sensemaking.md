# AI Sensemaking — Wise AI

**Question:** What is happening with AI, what futures could it produce, and how should we respond, particularly when we understand it as part of the wider metacrisis?

AI has crossed a significant threshold in capability and public use, and interpretations of where it leads range from extraordinary benefit to loss of control. This project distinguishes those claims, examines their assumptions and publishes what we find.

Work lives in [life-itself/ai-wise](https://github.com/life-itself/ai-wise) (private):

- `projects/wise-ai-briefing/`: the Wise AI inquiry and the briefing for Natasha (Plum Village), October 2026.
- `projects/share-question-tree/` and `projects/questions-for-ai-companies/`: smaller pieces.
- `site/`: the website to publish, a later phase.

Main epics: `ais-rpq` (question tree), `ais-61b` (pipeline to essays), `ais-q7s` (website).

The fuller framing (situation, complication, provisional issue tree, the metacrisis connection) is in Rufus's personal planning repo, `rufuspollock/planning`, `initiatives/ai-sense-making.md`; move it here when it is next revised.
