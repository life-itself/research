# Reason Commons

Tools and models for reasoning together towards a shared goal, with an AI as consultant, built on the Logical Thinking Process (LTP) and issue trees. One project across three repositories:

- [reasoncommons](https://github.com/life-itself/reasoncommons): LTP and issue-tree apps and explorations. Epic `reasoncommons-bnl` (explainers v2).
- [reason-commons-tui](https://github.com/life-itself/reason-commons-tui): the MVP, a local workspace that records goals, tests, actions and observations as attributed, versioned history, used from the command line or an AI agent.
- [2R-reason-commons](https://github.com/life-itself/2R-reason-commons): the Second Renaissance white papers modelled with LTP. The papers themselves stay under Publications.
