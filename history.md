## Phases

**1. Research Community (2016–2019).** An informal programme under Life Itself, with themes (Art/Earth/Tech, Open World) and calls on "wise" medicine, art and so on. The collective's origin. Sources: the 2016 initiative post and the 2016–present meeting minutes.

**2. Institute, renamed Labs (2019–2022).** From 2019 the work was formalised as the Life Itself Institute, with a core team (Liam Kavanagh, Theo Cox, Rufus) rather than a general community. Labs is the Institute renamed (Liam's "Goodbye Institute, Hello Labs": from think-tank to "think-do" experiments); it launched in June 2022 as a subsidiary for research and consulting on alternative economics and governance, and technology and society. 2022 outputs: web3 sensemaking guide, EU environmental data-sharing incentives, ecosystem mapping with new EU funding. Also in 2020–22 a "Research Program for a Wiser World" was drafted (no sign it was funded, guess). The 2025 SCQH calls 2019–22 "in abeyance" for the community side.

**3. The 2023 reboot.** Relaunched in January 2023 as an open community of 10–20 participants, called "Life Itself Research" (Rufus's own name history: Research Community, Institute, Labs, Research). Focal areas: inner development and cultural evolution; politics of paradigmatic change; systems transformation. Monthly open meetings, a forum, a June 2023 hackathon and a May 2024 one.

**4. Talks and the Sept 2024 retreat (2024–25).** A talks series ran from April 2024 to May 2025. One research retreat (France, September 2024) produced a manifesto, an SCQH and a position-paper skeleton. It covered developmental spaces, narrower than the whole collective.

**5. The 2025 diagnosis.** In January 2025 Rufus's SCQH named the problem: too little output and visibility, with unclear membership, governance and agenda. Jonah's guide to the research approach was approved by WhatsApp poll the same month. In the autumn, "Reflections on the Future" took stock, and Naeema drafted a circle framework that became the governance Pattern.

**6. Governance pattern (Nov 2025 – Apr 2026).** The Research Circle Pattern (stewardship circle, membership, cadence, sub-circles) was signed off in April 2026. A March 2026 mini-conference with IAM and an IAM scholar residency were planned in January but did not happen. Separately, the 2R movement side (Curators, the Oasis space) ran as its own group and the Oasis wound down in March 2026.

**7. Fellowship pivot and stewards (Jun–Oct 2026).** On 5 June stewards agreed to move from an open model to a structured fellowship (selection, defined outputs), but no written plan was found. Jonah has less capacity at the moment; Bob Bunge, as lead steward, runs a weekly speaker series, and the stewards Rufus, Jonah and Bob meet fortnightly. Comms moved WhatsApp → Discord; forum moving to Forum Magnum. Rufus calls it the "Seeds of Renaissance research group".

## Threads that run through it

- **Open vs structured:** open community (2023) → pattern with member terms (2026) → fellowship (proposed).
- **Output vs conversation:** the same gap (talks, not finished work) is named in 2025 and again in 2026.
- **Relation to the 2R movement:** research and 2R have been run as separate groups, with overlapping people.
- **Thin steward capacity:** a few volunteers (Jonah, Rufus, Naeema, now Bob).
