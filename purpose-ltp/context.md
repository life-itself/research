# Research group purpose: context for the LTP

The starting context for a Logical Thinking Process (LTP) on one question: **what is the research group for, and are we moving towards it?** The group has been called the Life Itself Research group, the Second Renaissance research group and now the Seeds of Renaissance research group.

Integrating the group into Seeds of Renaissance (branding, landing page, the SoR funnel) is out of scope here; the [research community project](../projects/2026-10-research-community/) covers that. That project's SCQ handed four issues to this LTP: energy, coordination and contribution; identity; steward capacity; budget.

- [pattern.md](../pattern.md): the governance charter, signed off April 2026 (Google Doc read 2026-10-22).
- [Where Next for 2025](https://research.seedsofrenaissance.org/posts/where-next-for-2025) (Jan 2025 SCQH; local copy at [posts/where-next-for-2025.md](../posts/where-next-for-2025.md), read 2026-10-22).

## Stated purpose (what the documents say)

**Pattern (Apr 2026).** "Explore paths to a radically wiser, weller world through a 'second renaissance'". The group works as both a scholarly inquiry and a living experiment, producing **public knowledge, practical models and collective understanding**.

- Framing question: what are the paths to a second renaissance? This breaks into what it is, how we get there, and why (what isn't working now).
- Objectives:
  1. Map the metacrisis.
  2. Study the emerging paradigms.
  3. Study the paths to them.
  4. Prototype and document experiments.
  5. Contribute to public discourse.

**Where Next for 2025 (Jan 2025).** There are two goals:

1. **Produce research**, even summary work, that builds towards the larger inquiry: what a second renaissance is, how it can come about, and how people can contribute.
2. **Build sensemaking capacity** in the group, so that its members can make sense of complex topics together effectively. Rufus flagged this goal as vague then.

Dissemination, to inform and influence the wider community, is also named. The group stays informal, made of people giving their own time.

## Situation

- **History.** The group ran informally from 2016, was dormant from 2019 to 2022, and restarted in January 2023. It had 10–20 participants in 2025.
- **January 2025 diagnosis.** The group produced too little output and got too little attention. Its vision, agenda, membership and governance were unclear.
- **Response.** The Pattern, written between October 2025 and April 2026, sets out a stewardship circle, one-year membership, fellows, sub-circles and a regular cadence. It mostly fixes governance and membership. It leaves the budget and the research agenda open.
- **June 2026.** The stewards agreed to move to a selective fellowship model, but there is no written plan for it. Jonah and Naeema took on less. Comms moved to Discord, and a move of the forum to Forum Magnum was planned (not yet done).
- **Now (October 2026).** The stewards are Rufus, Jonah and Bob Bunge. Bob runs the weekly Friday speaker series, with speakers booked to 4 December. Rufus and David Joseph are working on research "throughput" and on LTP trees.
- **Who is in it (Rufus, 2026-10-22).** Beyond the stewards, a broader group of about 8–15 participants meets regularly on the Friday call. Around them is a larger fringe of forum participants.

## Complication

- **Purpose vs activity.** The stated purpose is research outputs plus sensemaking capacity. In practice, the main regular activity is a speaker series. It is unclear what the series produces towards either goal.
- **The 2025 problems are mostly still open.** There is no output measure, no agreed research agenda beyond the speaker schedule, and no budget. Visibility is still weak: research.lifeitself.org "needs to be updated".
- **Energy and contribution.** The group runs on volunteers, so it is unclear how to get more energy, coordination and contribution. (Handed over from the SCQ.)
- **Steward capacity.** It is thin. Bob is lead steward; Jonah has less capacity at the moment. (Handed over from the SCQ.)
- **Direction.** There are two emphases in the group: local, practice-linked, aesthetic and philosophical work (Bob), and rigour and published outputs (the 2025 framing). How they fit together was open.
- **Identity.** The group's name and its relation to SoR and Life Itself are unsettled. (Handed over from the SCQ.)
- **Budget.** None is set. (Handed over from the SCQ.)

## Question

**What should the research group's purpose (goal) be now, what conditions must hold to achieve it, and is the group moving towards it?**

## Decided

- This thread covers purpose and progress towards it only. SoR integration and the landing page belong to the research community project.
- Use the `reason-commons` skill (`~/src/life-itself/reasoncommons/skills/reason-commons/`). The `ltp-project` skill was retired on 2026-10-21, and `reason-commons` replaces it.
- Start with a Goal Tree (goal → critical success factors → necessary conditions), then a Current Reality Tree on the gaps.
- This LTP starts from scratch. Don't reuse the older "2R Research LTP trees" (Rufus, 2026-10-22).
- The first draft goal is the two goals from 2025 (Rufus, 2026-10-22). The reasoning state is in [reason-commons.yaml](reason-commons.yaml).

## Hypotheses (not yet tested)

- The 2025 two-goal framing (research output plus sensemaking capacity) is still roughly right, but it has never been turned into conditions the group can check.
- The Pattern fixed governance but not purpose or throughput.
