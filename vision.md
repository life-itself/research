# Research group — vision and inspirations

A first pass, from sources read 2026-10-22. Not reviewed by Rufus or Jonah. Guesses are marked (guess).

## The vision, in the group's own words

- **Purpose (the Pattern, April 2026):** to explore paths to a radically wiser, weller world through a "second renaissance". Both a **scholarly inquiry** and a **living experiment**: research, dialogue and embodied practice that produce public knowledge, practical models and collective understanding. Framing question: *what are the paths to a second renaissance and an awakening society?* (What is it, how do we get there, why do we want to.) See [pattern.md](pattern.md).
- **Public page (lifeitself.org/research):** "a research collective exploring paths to a second renaissance and awakening societies. We produce reports, papers, methods and tools."
- **Research approach (Jonah's guide, approved Jan 2025):** research serves the 2R movement in four ways: the core narrative and theory of change, accessible summaries, mapping the ecosystem, and research as community-building. Open especially to researchers outside academia, with academic rigour. See the [sources](ref/sources.md).
- **Vision statement, as an open item in 2025:** "Where Next for 2025" asked for a vision stated two ways: by analogy to the **Frankfurt School**, and in terms of the sensemaking the group works towards ([post](posts/where-next-for-2025.md)).

## Inspirations: who we'd like to be like

An Excalidraw board, "Inspirations for a Life Itself Research Efforts / Second Renaissance Institute 2023", linked from the 2025 post. Working names on it: **Second Renaissance School**, **Metamodern Institute**, **Liminal College**. Picture: [inspirations board](assets/inspirations-board-2023.jpg) (screenshot, 2026-10-22).

| Inspiration | What it is |
|---|---|
| The Frankfurt School | Scholars around the Institute for Social Research (Frankfurt, 1923): critical theory, intellectuals working as a collective; the analogy named in the 2025 vision item |
| Bauhaus | German school of art, design and architecture (1919) bridging art and industry |
| Black Mountain College | Experimental liberal-arts college in North Carolina (1933), built on Dewey's educational philosophy, with art at the centre |
| Schumacher College | Ecology-focused short courses and degrees at Dartington, Devon |
| Gaia House (with a question mark on the board) | Retreat centre in Devon (guess: included for contemplative practice) |
| Santa Fe Institute | Independent, interdisciplinary complexity research, with a small resident faculty and a large rotating visiting community |
| École des Houches | Physics summer school (1951), a short residential school that grew into a lasting institution |

What the set has in common (guess): small, residential or highly connected communities; interdisciplinary; mixing inquiry with practice; and each shaped a field out of a network rather than a big organisation. That fits the group's own "scholarly inquiry and living experiment" line.
